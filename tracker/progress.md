## Conventions

- Only status `done` counts towards progress. `needs_revision` counts as incomplete.
- A topic is marked `done` only when the exercise passed, the quiz met the
  threshold, the concept was explained in the learner's own words at rating 2 or
  above, and no critical misconception is unresolved.
- Every number in this file must be traceable to a recorded assessment or a real
  command that was run.
